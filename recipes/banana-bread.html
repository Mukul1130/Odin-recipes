Banana Bread

Description

Looking for a moist banana bread recipe that never fails?
Your friends and family will ask for this never-dry banana bread again and again.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups white sugar
  • 1 cup butter, melted
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking-soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup sour scream
  • 1 cup chopped walnuts
  • 4 medium bananas, sliced

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
    Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Stir sugar and melted butter together in a large bowl. Add eggs and vanilla; mix well.
    Combine flour, baking soda, and salt; stir into butter mixture until smooth
  3. Fold in banana slices, sour cream, and walnuts; transfer into the prepared pan
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center of the loaf comes out clean, about 1 hour.
  5. Cool loaf in the pan for 10 minutes before inverting onto a wire rack to cool completely.
  6. Serve and enjoy!
